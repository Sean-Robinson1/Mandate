# Video script

The narration for the demo video (about 2 min 15 s). The recorded versions are in `Videos/Trusted Trade demo/`
on the presenting laptop: with voiceover, with captions, and plain. Times are from the start of the video, which
opens on a 3.5-second title card and ends on a closing card. To record your own voice, read each line over the
plain version, starting at its time.

**Title card:** Trusted Trade. A kitchen dashboard, and an ordering agent Valio Aimo can trust. Junction 2026, Valio Aimo challenge.

## 1. 0:05  Bistro Lumo's kitchen: stock, deliveries and orders on one screen

*On screen:* Supplies, as Elina: the To order and Arriving cards

> This is Bistro Lumo's kitchen. One screen shows what's in stock, what's on the way, and what needs ordering. Like most restaurants, they throw away a good chunk of the food they buy, and ordering eats hours every week.

## 2. 0:18  Ordering today: search 'lactose free milk', get a chocolate drink first

*On screen:* Search the catalog yourself: "lactose free milk" (a chocolate drink comes first)

> Here's how ordering works today. Search the catalogue for lactose free milk, and the top result is a chocolate drink. You'd still have to check every product yourself.

## 3. 0:28  The agent ordered the cream itself. The cheese waits for the owner

*On screen:* The headline and To order card: cream ordered by the agent, grated cheese waiting for Jussi

> So we gave the kitchen a purchasing agent. This morning's stock count showed cream and grated cheese running low. The agent ordered the cream straight away, because it's within the owner's limits. The cheese needs his okay first.

## 4. 0:40  Salmon about to go to waste: the app suggests a 20% special

*On screen:* The salmon waste alert with its suggested 20% special

> It also watches for waste. This salmon won't get used before its date, so the app suggests a twenty percent special on the pan-fried salmon. The kitchen still makes money on every plate.

## 5. 0:52  Over €600, the agent has to ask the owner

*On screen:* Ask the agent: "We need 400 litres of cream by Tuesday"; the request waits for Jussi

> Now let's ask for something bigger: four hundred litres of cream. That's over six hundred euros, so the agent can't order it alone. It finds the product, checks the allergens, gets a signed price from Valio Aimo, and asks the owner.

## 6. 1:05  The owner approves with his own signature. Valio Aimo checks it

*On screen:* Switch to Jussi; Approvals; review the cream order; Approve and order

> Jussi, the owner, gets the full picture: the price compared to what he normally pays, why the agent asked, and which dishes use it. He approves, and his approval is signed with his own key, so Valio Aimo can check it really came from him.

## 7. 1:19  One click puts the salmon on special

*On screen:* Supplies: Run the special on the salmon alert

> While he's here, he puts the salmon on special. One click.

## 8. 1:24  A compromised agent tries seven tricks. Valio Aimo stops all of them

*On screen:* Try to cheat: Run the cheating agent; 7 of 7 refused

> But what if the agent goes rogue? Here a compromised agent tries seven tricks: changing prices, reusing old offers, raising its own limits, approving its own orders. Valio Aimo catches every one of them.

## 9. 1:37  Valio Aimo can pause an agent. Its next order is turned down

*On screen:* Valio Aimo's operator page: Pause agent; back in the kitchen, "20 litres of milk by Tuesday" is turned down

> Valio Aimo also has an off switch. Once the agent is paused, its next order is turned down, and the kitchen can see exactly why.

## 10. 1:48  How much food is wasted, what it costs, and why

*On screen:* Food cost and waste: the tiles and "Where the waste comes from"

> Back in the kitchen, the waste page shows how much food is thrown away, what it costs each week, and where it comes from.

## 11. 1:56  Any agent can order, but only within rules a person signed

*On screen:* Audit log: Chain verified

> And everything that happened, by the agent, by people, and by Valio Aimo, is kept in a log that can't be quietly changed. Any agent can order from Valio Aimo, but only within the rules a person has signed.

**Closing card:** Any agent can order from Valio Aimo, but only within rules a person signed. Rules in code,
signed offers, orders and approvals, an off switch, every step on the record.
